@Handle Auto‑Tagger for Buffer

Privacy

Last updated November 2026.

What the app accesses

When you connect Buffer, Handle Auto-Tagger asks for three permissions: account:read (your organizations and connected channels), posts:read (the text of posts you have already sent, used to find the accounts you tag) and posts:write (to add the posts you compose to your Buffer queue, schedule or drafts).

Where your data lives

Your Buffer access tokens are stored only in an encrypted, HTTP-only cookie in your browser. They are never readable by page scripts and are not saved on our servers.

Post text passes through the app's server on its way from Buffer to your browser. It is not stored, logged or shared. The handle directory built from it is saved only in your browser's local storage, and you can export it as a CSV at any time.

The app has no database, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and does not sell or share any data.

Removing access

Click Disconnect in the app to clear your session. You can also revoke the app's access from your Buffer account settings. Clearing your browser's site data removes the saved directory.
